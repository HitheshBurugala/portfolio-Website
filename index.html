Hi, I'm Hithesh Burugala. I'm a Data Engineer focused on building reliable, scalable, and analytics-ready data solutions that transform complex, multi-source data into meaningful business insights.

I specialize in building data pipelines, performing data integration and transformation, ensuring data quality, and developing business intelligence solutions. I work extensively with Azure, AWS ,SQL, Python, PySpark, ETL/ELT workflows, and Power BI to turn raw data into structured and actionable datasets.

Currently, I'm a Data Engineer at Jarir Investment, where I work closely with Investment Managers, Investment Analysts, and business stakeholders to build and maintain Azure-based data engineering workflows. My work involves ingesting, transforming, validating, reconciling, and standardizing financial and investment data across multiple sources.

I also work with investment and portfolio data, including stocks, sectors, industries, countries, exchanges, currencies, securities, and portfolio holdings. I develop Power BI dashboards and analytical datasets that provide visibility into portfolio positioning, Top 10 holdings, stock and sector concentration, geographic exposure, portfolio entries and exits, and historical trends to support investment analysis and decision-making.

I'm also interested in automation and AI-assisted development. I use tools such as Claude, and the Claude Excel Add-in to accelerate SQL, Python and Excel development, troubleshoot data-processing issues, automate repetitive research workflows, and extract structured information from external sources into usable datasets.

Previously, at NIQ (NielsenIQ), I worked on enterprise data engineering and analytics solutions involving ETL/ELT, data integration, data transformation, data quality, reconciliation, and production data pipelines. I worked with data from multiple sources and helped build reliable, analytics-ready datasets for downstream reporting and business applications.
